# US Retail Best-Practice Checklist

Use this list to decide **what to look for**. It contains no findings. Source every claim at research time.

| Area | What to check | Best evidence (tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Omnichannel | Unified inventory view, ship-from-store, endless aisle | Analyst notes 🟢 · 10-K 🟢 · trade press 🟡 |
| BOPIS / curbside | Pickup SLA, share of digital orders picked up in store | 10-K / earnings call 🟢 · trade press 🟡 |
| Order management (OMS) | Platform used, distributed order routing | Named-customer case study 🟡 · vendor site 🔴 |
| Inventory | RFID, cycle counts, shrink programs | NRF shrink survey 🟢 · 10-K risk factors 🟢 |
| Loyalty | Tiers, member share of sales, paid vs. free | 10-K / investor day 🟢 · peer comparison 🟡 |
| Personalization / CDP | Data platform, first-party data use | Analyst 🟢 · case study 🟡 · vendor 🔴 |
| Retail media network | Network exists, partners, self-serve tools | Trade press 🟡 · company press release 🔴 |
| Returns | Window, policy for used or opened goods, return fraud controls | Policy page (as a FACT about the policy) · NRF returns report 🟢 |
| Store ops | Workforce tools, task management, mobile POS | Trade press 🟡 · vendor 🔴 |
| Clienteling | Associate apps, appointment booking | Trade press 🟡 · vendor 🔴 |
| Payments | BNPL, wallets, self-checkout | 10-K 🟢 · trade press 🟡 |

## Primary sources to check first
- **SEC EDGAR** 10-K / 10-Q / 8-K for public retailers: https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/
- **NRF** research and surveys: https://nrf.com/research-insights
- **US Census** retail trade and e-commerce data: https://www.census.gov/retail/index.html

> Policy pages: a retailer's own policy page is the authority on **what its policy says**. Record it as a FACT with LOW tier per the tiering rule, and note in the statement that it is the retailer's published policy.
