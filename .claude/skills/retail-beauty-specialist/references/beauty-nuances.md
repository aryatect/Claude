# Beauty Specialty Retail Checklist

These dimensions set beauty specialty retail (Ulta Beauty, Sephora, Sally Beauty, Bluemercury and others) apart from general retail. The list says what to check. It contains no findings.

## Customer experience

| Area | What to check |
|---|---|
| Testers and hygiene | Tester policy, sanitizing practices, single-use applicators |
| Sampling | Free samples with orders, deluxe minis, sample-to-purchase programs |
| Shade match / skin analysis | In-store devices, app tools, AI skin diagnostics |
| Virtual try-on (AR) | Product categories covered, vendor (e.g., ModiFace, Perfect Corp), app vs. web |
| Loyalty | Beauty Insider (Sephora), Ulta Beauty Rewards: tiers, point value, member share of sales |
| Shop-in-shops | Sephora at Kohl's, Ulta Beauty at Target: store count, assortment, loyalty linkage |
| Services | Salon, brow, piercing, makeup application, booking tools |
| Beauty advisors | Training, certification, clienteling apps, commission model |
| Returns | Policy on used or opened cosmetics, return window, abuse controls |
| Assortment | Prestige vs. mass mix, brand exclusives, "clean" assortment programs |

## Regulatory and compliance (US)

| Area | What to check | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| MoCRA (Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act of 2022) | Facility registration, product listing, adverse-event reporting, safety substantiation, upcoming rules | FDA: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics |
| Labeling | Ingredient declaration, net contents, warning statements | FDA, 21 CFR 701 and 740: https://www.ecfr.gov |
| "Clean" / "natural" / "green" claims | Substantiation of environmental claims | FTC Green Guides: https://www.ftc.gov |
| California rules | Toxic-Free Cosmetics Act (AB 2762), Prop 65 warnings, Cosmetic Fragrance and Flavor Ingredient Right to Know Act | https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov · https://oehha.ca.gov |
| Washington rules | Toxic-Free Cosmetics Act ingredient bans | https://app.leg.wa.gov · https://ecology.wa.gov |
| PFAS in cosmetics | State bans (several states) and the federal MoCRA PFAS report | State legislatures · FDA |
| Animal testing | State cruelty-free cosmetics laws | State legislatures |

**Version and date callout:** regulations change by effective date. For every regulatory claim, record the effective date and the rule or bill number in `as_of_version` (for example, `MoCRA facility registration — FDA enforcement from 2024-07-01`).
